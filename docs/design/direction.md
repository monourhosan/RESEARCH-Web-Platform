# Design direction: research starts with people

The primary audience is research buyers at NGOs, development organizations, public institutions, and businesses. Their task is to understand the offer and start a research conversation.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque for complete headings, Source Sans 3 for readable body and controls, Caveat only for a few handwritten notes beside the illustrations. All fonts are locally hosted with their OFL licenses.

## Layout study

Candidate A: retain the boxed illustration, equal service cards, and numbered heading labels. Rejected: it preserves the template rhythm the user has identified.

Candidate B: an open research notebook, with a large survey conversation next to the opening message, quieter open service columns, and a large focus-group scene beside a plain explanation of qualitative research. Chosen: the subject is immediately recognizable and the drawing supplies the personality.

Everything is left aligned except the handwritten annotations, which follow the scene rather than a rigid card template.

## Critique before implementation

The earlier warm background, italic end-of-headline, numbered labels above unrelated sections, tiny uppercase text, and repeated equal cards read as a default consulting template. Remove those treatments. Keep brand blue and teal, but use white as the base and give the illustrations room. Number only the research process, where the order is meaningful. Do not add fake client logos, biographies, testimonials, or research results.

## Motion

One small entrance sequence for the hero drawing and a drawn underline. Otherwise animate responses to user actions: FAQ expansion, research-step changes, regional selections, and opening navigation or the proposal dialog. No continuous floating, parallax, custom cursor, or repeated scroll reveals. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and preserve keyboard focus.
