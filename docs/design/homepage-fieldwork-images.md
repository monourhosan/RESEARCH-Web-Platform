# Homepage fieldwork image set

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These photorealistic illustrations depict example data collection in Bangladesh. They are not photographs of actual Mirror Metrics staff, clients, or research participants. Original stock photography remains in place on other routes.

The generated originals were visually reviewed and encoded as WebP for the website. Natural image colors are retained; the website UI continues to use blue, white, green, and red.

Final prompt:

She asks a household survey question and records the response on a tablet. The participant is engaged and gesturing naturally. Corrugated tin wall, wooden doorway and household items give understated lived-in context. Both adults clearly visible, waist-up to full seated composition, interaction slightly right of center with environmental breathing space around them, warm daylight spilling in from the courtyard. Respectful, dignified and ordinary.
